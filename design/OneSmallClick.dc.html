1lHow to play
HOW TO PLAY
One probe.
Everyone's taps.
1 Tap THRUST
Every tap from every player pushes the same probe toward Mars.
2 Keep the signal alive
If the timer hits zero the probe goes quiet. Anyone can Ping once a day to reset it.
3 Vote the route
One crew vote a day: faster, safer, or a detour for a postcard.
4 Upgrade your thrusters
They keep pushing while you're away, for up to 8 hours.
The big date on the home screen moves with all of us. More play, sooner arrival.
